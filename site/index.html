What PRYN does

Three steps. No mystery.

01

Looks at your system

Reads Linux device state and the services that manage networking, Bluetooth and audio.

02

Checks the evidence

Compares what it can observe against specific diagnostic rules. It does not guess.

03

Explains what it found

Shows pass, warning, problem or unknown results in a readable report or JSON.

THREE AREAS

One tool. Three checks.

Wi-Fi

Interfaces, radio state, drivers, IP addresses, default routes and configured DNS.

Bluetooth

Controllers, radio blocks, BlueZ availability, adapter power, paired devices and connection state.

Audio

ALSA cards, audio servers, default devices, mute, volume, ports and exposed profiles.

BUILT WITH RESTRAINT

Diagnostics, not a system repair tool.

PRYN is deliberately read-only. It gives you evidence so you can decide what to do next.

No automatic fixesIt does not change settings or restart services.
No background daemonIt runs when you ask it to run.
Unknown stays unknownMissing evidence is never presented as a healthy result.
